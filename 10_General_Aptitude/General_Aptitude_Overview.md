# General Aptitude (GA) — Complete GATE Syllabus & Overview

**Mandatory GATE Weightage:** 15 Marks (Common to all engineering disciplines)

---

## 📌 Sections & Topic Breakdown

### Section 1: Quantitative Aptitude
* **Data Interpretation:** Data graphs (bar graphs, pie charts, histograms, line graphs), tables.
* **Numerical Computation & Estimation:**
  * Ratios, Proportions, Percentages.
  * Powers, Exponents, Logarithms.
  * Permutations and Combinations, Elementary Probability.
* **Applied Mathematics:**
  * Profit and Loss, Simple & Compound Interest.
  * Time and Work, Pipes & Cisterns.
  * Time, Speed and Distance, Relative speed.

### Section 2: Analytical Aptitude
* **Logic & Deduction:** Syllogisms, Truth-teller vs Liar puzzles.
* **Analogy & Numerical Reasoning:** Number series, Pattern completions, Coding-Decoding.
* **Blood Relations & Direction Sense.**

### Section 3: Spatial Aptitude
* Transformation of shapes: Translation, Rotation, Mirroring, Scaling.
* Paper folding, cutting, and 2D/3D visualization.
* Assembling and grouping pieces.

### Section 4: Verbal Aptitude
* **English Grammar:** Tenses, Subject-Verb Agreement, Prepositions, Conjunctions.
* **Vocabulary:** Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms and Phrases, Contextual word usage.
* **Reading Comprehension & Critical Reasoning:** Passage inferences, Identifying assumptions and conclusions.
