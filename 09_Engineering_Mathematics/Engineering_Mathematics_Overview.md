# Engineering Mathematics — Complete GATE Syllabus & Overview

**Typical GATE Weightage:** ~13 – 15 Marks

---

## 📌 Core Modules & Key High-Yield Topics

### Module 1: Discrete Mathematics (Highest Weightage)
* **Mathematical Logic:**
  * Propositional Logic: Truth tables, Tautology, Contradiction, Logical equivalences.
  * Predicate Logic: First-order logic, Universal ($\forall$) and Existential ($\exists$) Quantifiers.
  * Inference rules (Modus Ponens, Modus Tollens).
* **Set Theory & Relations:**
  * Sets, Power set, Cartesian product.
  * Relations: Reflexive, Symmetric, Anti-symmetric, Transitive, Equivalence relations, Equivalence classes.
  * Partial Order Relations, Posets, Hasse Diagrams, Lattices, Boolean Algebras.
* **Combinatorics:**
  * Permutations and Combinations ($^nP_r, ^nC_r$).
  * Pigeonhole Principle.
  * Generating Functions and Recurrence Relations.
* **Graph Theory:**
  * Connected components, Bipartite graphs, Handshaking Lemma.
  * Euler Paths/Circuits, Hamiltonian Paths/Cycles.
  * Planar Graphs (Euler's formula: $V - E + R = 2$), Graph Coloring (Chromatic number).
  * Trees: Properties, spanning trees, Cayley's formula ($n^{n-2}$).

### Module 2: Linear Algebra
* **Matrices & Determinants:** Rank of a matrix, System of Linear Equations ($AX = B$: Unique, Infinite, No solution).
* **Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors:**
  * Properties of eigenvalues (Trace = Sum of eigenvalues, Determinant = Product of eigenvalues).
  * Cayley-Hamilton Theorem ($A$ satisfies its own characteristic equation).
  * Diagonalization and powers of matrices.

### Module 3: Calculus
* Limits, Continuity, and Differentiability.
* Mean Value Theorems (Rolle's Theorem, Cauchy's & Lagrange's MVT).
* Maxima and Minima (Single variable and multi-variable saddle points).
* Definite and Improper Integrals.

### Module 4: Probability & Statistics
* Axioms of Probability, Conditional Probability, Multiplication theorem.
* Bayes' Theorem and Law of Total Probability.
* Random Variables: Discrete (Binomial, Poisson) vs Continuous (Uniform, Exponential, Normal / Gaussian).
* Expectation, Variance, Standard Deviation, Covariance.
